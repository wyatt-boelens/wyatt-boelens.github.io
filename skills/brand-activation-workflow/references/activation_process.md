# Brand Activation — Process Reference (Example)

Full detail on the activation process, platform specifics, settings explanations, and tone guidelines. Read this before generating any output.

---

## Pre-demo setup (seller side)

Before every demo:
1. Research the brand's public website for product info, pricing, and positioning
2. Identify any active public offers to anchor a reasonable discount range (the brand can adjust after setup)
3. Build the brand profile and perks page
4. Create login credentials to share at or after the demo

By the time the follow-up email goes out, the account is usually already live — the seller just needs to confirm credentials have been sent.

## Full activation sequence (post-demo)

### Step 1 — Account credentials
Share the brand login at: https://loopline.example/for-brands/login
Designate an account administrator — the main contact, someone on their team, or their affiliate manager.
The seller can handle most of the setup with a few simple instructions if the brand prefers minimal involvement.

### Step 2 — Primary platform connection (primary technical step)
Brand sends a partnership request through the primary platform.
Assigned Partner ID: **{{partner_id}}**
Once connected, the seller accepts the request and begins platform-side setup.
This is the single most important brand action — if it doesn't happen, nothing else moves forward.

### Step 3 — Landing page
Two options:
- **Existing landing page (~5 minutes):** brand provides the URL they already use; seller configures it.
- **Custom landing page (~25 minutes):** brand creates a subscriber-specific page.

Best practice:
- Evergreen (subscribers don't lose access over time)
- Headline customized for partner subscribers
- Clear subscriber-exclusive offer (discount, extended trial, free gift, exclusive bundle)

Example headlines:
- "Exclusive Offer for Our Partners' Subscribers"
- "Welcome! Your Exclusive [Brand] Offer"
- "Special Offer for Verified Subscribers"
- "Unlock Your Exclusive [Brand] Perk"
- "A Subscriber-Only Offer, Just for You"

### Step 4 — Tracking verification (seller side)
Verify:
- Tracking link is live and not expired
- Subscriber flow is correct: landing page → offer/selection page → checkout → discount auto-applied
- Confirmed with the brand (e.g. a fictional example: "Verdant Kitchen") that the coupon auto-applies at final checkout

### Step 5 — Platform settings review
Two key settings to flag for every brand:

**Allow Sales** — the seller can drive subscriber traffic to the brand's landing page from partner perks pages. The acquisition side. Almost always enabled by default.

**Enable Offers** — the brand's own subscribers can see exclusive partner offers from other network brands on their perks page. The retention side. Many brands don't enable this initially — worth flagging as an upsell opportunity.

Plain-English explanation for the follow-up email: "One thing worth knowing — there are two settings in your account that control how you participate in the network. 'Allow Sales' means your brand can appear on partner perks pages (acquisition). 'Enable Offers' means your own subscribers see exclusive partner offers as part of their membership (retention). Both are worth enabling for maximum value, but totally your call."

**Auto-accept vs. manual review toggle:**
- Auto-accept: new partner requests are approved automatically, brand goes live immediately
- Manual review: brand reviews each partner request before accepting
- Default is auto-accept
- Worth flagging so brands know they have control over which partners they associate with

### Step 6 — Live confirmation
Once the primary platform is connected and the landing page is verified:
- Brand is live across relevant partner perks pages
- Performance analytics visible in the brand dashboard: partner-level traffic/conversion data, and high-level subscriber analytics
- Seller follows up over time with performance updates, partner recommendations, and optimization tips as the program grows

## Platform-specific notes

### Primary Platform (standard path)
Most common. Full process as described above.

### Secondary Platform
Same flow as the primary platform. Integration is in progress — position as available soon, don't block the deal. Set up the account now and confirm connection timing separately.

### Other platforms
Network coverage is actively expanding. Typical timeline: a few weeks, depending on development priorities. Keep the deal warm, don't lose momentum — there's no reason setup can't start now.

### No affiliate platform / direct setup
Flag internally. May require a seller-hosted affiliate setup or a direct integration discussion. Don't generate specific technical steps for this scenario — flag it as a pending item instead.

## Commission guidance

General principle, not a fixed number: match whatever the brand's typical affiliate payout already is, rather than pushing them above or below their existing standard. Rates vary meaningfully by brand and margin — there's no single "right" number, which is exactly why matching their existing rate (rather than anchoring to a house standard) is the right default.

## Email tone guidelines

Human and warm, not corporate. Write as if the seller is a smart, helpful colleague who genuinely wants this to go smoothly — not a vendor checking boxes.

Avoid:
- "As per our conversation"
- "Please don't hesitate to reach out"
- "We're thrilled to have you on board"
- "Looking forward to a fruitful partnership"
- Excessive exclamation marks
- Walls of bullet points with no connective tissue

Use:
- The brand's and contact's actual names throughout
- Specific references to what was discussed (the offer, the platform, the team members)
- Plain-English explanations of technical steps
- A single, clear primary next step at the top
- A warm but not gushing close

## Open questions / objection handling

**"How does subscriber verification actually work?"**
In practice, very few non-subscribers attempt to access offers, since they're surfaced through subscriber-only channels — member emails, in-app experiences, subscriber pages. No manual review or login checks needed at the standard tier; automated verification is available at a higher tier for brands that want it.

**"What if partners aren't a good fit for our brand?"**
The brand controls which partners they associate with via the manual review toggle or guardrails in account settings — full control over appearances.

**"We're not on the primary platform — can we still participate?"**
Yes — network coverage is actively expanding to additional platforms, typically within a few weeks. Setup can begin now regardless.

**"What does the setup actually require from our side?"**
Minimal. Primary action: send a partnership request through the platform. Secondary: provide a landing page URL. The seller handles the rest. Total time: 5–25 minutes depending on whether an existing or custom landing page is used.
